# Privacy policy

D1 Lab evaluates text, photos and recordings using models on your device. It does not require an account.

## Inputs and results

The app does not send your input text, instructions, photos, recordings or results to a developer server or external AI service. Decisions run on your iPhone or Mac. The app has no advertising or analytics features.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores downloaded models, imported photos and resized images, recordings, the most recent 100 history entries, saved tasks and settings such as your language preference. Saved tasks include your text and decision criteria. Media from removed attachments or older history entries may remain until you delete it.

Depending on your operating system and backup settings, saved data may be included in device backups. Deleting data from the app does not remove existing backups or files you have exported.

## Model downloads

Model downloads connect to Hugging Face and its delivery services. Connection information such as your IP address is available to those services. Download requests do not include your decision text, photos or recordings. After downloading, you can run decisions offline.

Hugging Face privacy policy: https://huggingface.co/privacy

## Camera, photos and microphone

The app asks for operating system permission when you take a photo, select a photo or record audio. It saves a copy of imported media and does not change your original photos or source files. Recordings last up to 30 seconds. You can change permissions in your iPhone or Mac settings.

## Deleting data

Deleting an entry from History removes that entry and media unused by other history entries or the current input.

In Models → Saved data, you can remove unused media or delete all history, photos and recordings. Deleting all history and media also removes the current attachment. Models and saved tasks are kept.

Use Delete model files to remove downloaded models and partial downloads. Delete saved tasks from the task list. Deletion of app data cannot be undone. The operating system manages app storage and backups after uninstallation.

## Exporting data

If you save or copy a run as JSON, it includes input text, instructions, answers, measurements and information such as media storage locations. It does not include the photo or recording itself. You manage exported files and any copies you share.

## Contact and changes

Contact us through GitHub Issues in the public repository. Do not attach inputs or media containing personal information to public reports. This policy will be updated when data handling changes.
